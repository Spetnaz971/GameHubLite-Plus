What Are GHLP Cores?

The intelligent engine inside GameHubLite Plus that makes your games run better.

GHLP Cores is a smart performance engine built into GameHubLite Plus. It monitors what your game needs second-by-second and adjusts how your phone's processor, graphics chip, and memory work together. The result: less stuttering, better efficiency, and longer sessions — without you having to fiddle with a single setting.

Think of it like a smart co-pilot for your phone's processor. It watches game scenes in real time — when an explosion happens, it allocates more power. When you are just walking through a quiet area, it eases off to save battery and reduce strain. All of this happens automatically, thousands of times per second.
