What Are GHLP Cores?

The intelligent engine inside GameHubLite Plus that makes your games run better.

GHLP Cores is a smart performance engine built into GameHubLite Plus. It monitors what your game needs second-by-second and adjusts how your phone's processor, graphics chip, and memory work together. The result: less stuttering, cooler temperatures, and longer sessions — without you having to fiddle with a single setting.

Think of it like a smart co-pilot for your phone's processor. It watches game scenes in real time — when an explosion happens, it allocates more power. When you are just walking through a quiet area, it eases off to save battery and keep things cool. All of this happens automatically, thousands of times per second.

Thermal Management

How GHLP Cores protects your device when things heat up.

When you game, your phone's processor generates heat. Too much heat means the phone has to slow down to protect itself — that is what causes frame drops and stuttering. GHLP Cores takes a smarter approach: instead of waiting for things to get hot and then slamming the brakes, it actively manages heat in real time by making tiny adjustments to keep performance high and temperatures safe. GHLP Cores 3.2 introduces adaptive thermal profiles — Safe, Balanced, and Aggressive — automatically selected based on your device's temperature. This means the engine intelligently chooses the right thermal strategy moment by moment, keeping you in the game longer without uncomfortable heat. The result is longer gaming sessions at higher frame rates without your device feeling like a hand warmer.

Automatic FPS Adjustment

GHLP Cores continuously monitors the gap between current temperature and the safety limit. When that gap shrinks, it makes micro-adjustments to the frame rate — so small you won't notice them individually. Rather than a sudden drop from 60 FPS to 30 FPS (which feels terrible), GHLP Cores might step down from 60 to 57 to 53 to 48 — each step barely perceptible, keeping the game feeling smooth while temperatures stabilize. Once things cool down, it steps back up just as gently.

This is completely automatic. You do not need to set anything up. Just launch your game and GHLP Cores handles the rest.
